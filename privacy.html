Privacy Policy

ZenEd ("we," "us," or "our") operates an AI-powered educational platform that includes study tools, performance tracking, and a one-on-one tutoring marketplace. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights when you use ZenEd at kunalkaiser.github.io/zened/ (the "Service").

By using ZenEd, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. What We Collect

All users — information you provide when creating an account:

  • Name (first name or full name)
  • Email address
  • Role (student, teacher, or parent)
  • Grade level (for students)
  • Password (stored encrypted — we never see it in plain text)
  • State or region (optional, for exam relevance)

Students — information you provide while using ZenEd:

  • Grade entries (subject, topic, score, assignment type)
  • Classroom codes (if joining a class)
  • Questions submitted to the AI assistant

Tutors — additional information collected:

  • Profile information (subjects offered, biography, stated rates)
  • Availability information
  • Payment account information (processed through third-party provider — see Section 5)

Information collected automatically:

  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on ZenEd
  • IP address (used for security purposes only)
Data we do NOT collect: Social Security numbers, student ID numbers, official transcripts, IEP or 504 documentation, discipline records, physical addresses, or any government-issued ID information. We intentionally keep our data footprint minimal.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Calculate readiness scores and predicted Regents exam scores
  • Generate personalized study recommendations
  • Allow teachers to monitor classroom readiness (only for students who joined their class)
  • Allow parents to view their linked child's readiness data
  • Power the ZenEd AI assistant to answer questions
  • Facilitate tutoring session scheduling and payment processing
  • Retain tutoring session metadata for billing and compliance purposes
  • Improve and maintain the platform
  • Send important service updates (not marketing emails)

We do not use student data for advertising, marketing, or to build profiles for commercial purposes. We do not use student data to train AI models.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.

Your data is shared only in these limited circumstances:

  • With your teacher — if you join their classroom, they can see your subject readiness scores and averages. They cannot see individual grade entries.
  • With your parent — if a parent links to your account, they can see your readiness scores across subjects.
  • With Tutors (tutoring marketplace users only) — limited profile information (such as first name and subject area) may be visible to facilitate tutoring sessions. Academic data remains private unless you explicitly share it within a session. Tutor profile information (name, subjects, biography, and availability) is visible to students seeking tutoring.
  • With Supabase — our database provider stores your data securely. Supabase is SOC 2 Type 2 compliant.
  • With Anthropic — questions submitted to the AI assistant are processed by Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate responses. We do not use student data to train our own AI models. Anthropic does not use API-submitted data to train public models. Users should avoid submitting sensitive personal information in chat messages.
  • With payment processors — payment information is processed by third-party providers (see Section 5). We do not store full credit card numbers.
  • When required by law — we may disclose information if required by a valid legal process.

Tutoring sessions conducted through third-party platforms (e.g., Zoom, Google Meet) are governed by the privacy policies of those providers. ZenEd does not record or store video or audio content from tutoring sessions.

Tutoring session metadata (date, duration, payment amount) may be retained for billing and compliance purposes.

4. AI Services & Data

ZenEd uses artificial intelligence to provide readiness estimates, predicted exam scores, study recommendations, and automated responses to questions you submit.

When you use AI-powered features:

  • Text you submit to the AI assistant is transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API to generate a response.
  • We do not use your submitted questions or academic data to train AI models.
  • Anthropic does not use API-submitted data to train its public models.
  • Do not submit sensitive personal information (full name, student ID, address, etc.) in AI chat messages.

AI-generated content — including readiness scores, score predictions, and study recommendations — may contain inaccuracies. Always verify important academic information with teachers or official sources.

5. Payments & Billing Information

If you purchase a subscription or tutoring session, payment information is processed by our third-party payment provider (such as Stripe). We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers. Billing data is used solely to process transactions and manage your subscription or tutoring payments.

Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled prior to the renewal date. You may cancel at any time through your account settings. New York law requires clear disclosure of auto-renewal terms, and we provide that disclosure at the point of purchase.

Refund eligibility, if any, is described at the time of purchase. For tutoring sessions, refund terms are subject to the individual Tutor's stated cancellation policy.

Tutors who receive payments through the platform are responsible for reporting and paying any applicable taxes on income earned. ZenEd may issue tax documentation as required by law.

6. Tutoring Marketplace Data

ZenEd operates a one-on-one tutoring marketplace that facilitates connections, scheduling, and payments between students and independent Tutors. Sessions take place on third-party platforms (such as Zoom or Google Meet) selected by the Tutor and student.

What we collect related to tutoring:

  • Tutor profile information (name, subjects, biography, rates, availability)
  • Booking and scheduling information
  • Payment and transaction records (processed through third-party providers)
  • Session metadata (date, duration, payment amount) for billing and compliance

What we do NOT collect:

  • Video or audio recordings of tutoring sessions
  • Content discussed during off-platform sessions
  • Chat logs from Zoom, Google Meet, or other third-party tools

Tutor profile information (name, subjects offered, biography) is visible to students using the tutoring marketplace. Student academic data is never publicly displayed and is not shared with Tutors without explicit student action.

⚠️ Parents: ZenEd does not monitor tutoring sessions conducted on third-party platforms. Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise or be aware of tutoring sessions involving minors. We recommend sessions with minors take place in appropriate, supervised environments.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

ZenEd is designed for high school students and takes children's privacy seriously.

Users under 13: We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn that a user under 13 has provided personal information without parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.

Users 13–17: We encourage parents and guardians to be aware of their child's use of ZenEd. Parents can link to their child's account to monitor readiness scores. For tutoring involving minors, parents are responsible for supervising or monitoring sessions as they deem appropriate — ZenEd does not monitor off-platform sessions.

If you are a parent and believe your child's account should be removed, contact us at hello@zened.app. We will respond within 5 business days.

8. Student Data & FERPA

ZenEd operates as an independent consumer platform — students sign up directly, not through their school. In this model, ZenEd is not subject to FERPA requirements as a school vendor. However, we voluntarily apply FERPA-aligned principles to all student data.

When ZenEd enters into a written agreement with a school or district to provide services, we act as a "school official" with legitimate educational interests under FERPA and process student data solely on behalf of the educational institution. We enter into written Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with school partners that define our data handling responsibilities, deletion timelines, and breach notification obligations.

In both consumer and school-contract contexts, we apply the following principles:

  • Student grade data is used only to provide the ZenEd service to that student
  • We do not share student data with third parties for commercial purposes
  • Students (and parents of minors) can request deletion of their data at any time
  • Teachers only see data for students who voluntarily joined their classroom
  • We do not collect student ID numbers, official transcripts, IEP documentation, or discipline records
ZenEd is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a contractor of the New York City Department of Education or any NYC public school, unless a written agreement is in place.

9. Data Storage, Security & Retention

Your data is stored on Supabase's secure cloud infrastructure. We use the following security practices:

  • All passwords are hashed using industry-standard encryption — we never store plain-text passwords
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
  • Row-level security policies ensure users can only access their own data
  • Teacher and parent access is limited to only the data they are authorized to see
  • Payment data is handled exclusively by third-party processors and is not stored on our servers

Data retention: We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Service or comply with legal obligations. Inactive accounts may be deleted after an extended period of inactivity. Users may request deletion at any time. Upon deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days except where retention is required by law or for legitimate billing/compliance purposes.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your ZenEd account.

10. Security Incidents & Breach Notification

In the event of a data security incident affecting personal information, we will notify affected users within a reasonable timeframe as required by applicable law. For school or district partners operating under a written agreement, we commit to breach notification within 72 hours of discovery, consistent with contractual obligations and applicable regulations.

If you suspect unauthorized access to your account or data, contact us immediately at hello@zened.app.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct — update or correct inaccurate information in your account
  • Delete — request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the Service at any time
  • Data portability — request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@zened.app. We will respond within 10 business days. Account deletion requests will be processed within 30 days.

If you are a parent or guardian exercising rights on behalf of a minor, we treat those requests as our highest priority.

12. Cookies

ZenEd uses minimal cookies and browser storage:

  • Authentication cookies — to keep you logged in between sessions (set by Supabase Auth)
  • Session storage — temporary data used while you're actively using the platform

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, which will log you out of ZenEd.

ZenEd uses the following third-party services to operate:
