C
Claim
State your answer directly. Don't start with "I think." One sentence that answers what's asked.
E
Evidence
Use specific data from the graph, table, or model. Include numbers. "The graph shows" alone earns zero.
R

Explain WHY your evidence proves your claim using biology. This is where most students lose the point.
