Teacher Independence & Conflict Policy

This platform is an independently created educational resource developed outside of the creator’s school employment.

To maintain clear professional boundaries and comply with school district conflict-of-interest policies:

  • The platform is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any school or district, including the New York City Department of Education.
  • The platform is not required for any class, assignment, or grading activity.
  • The creator does not promote or require use of the platform during classroom instruction.
  • Students currently enrolled in classes taught by the creator are not solicited or recruited to use the platform.
  • Any use of the platform by such students is voluntary and independent of school coursework.
  • The creator does not provide tutoring services to students currently enrolled in their classes, whether paid or unpaid.
  • All development, operation, and communication related to the platform occur outside of school hours and without use of school resources.

This policy exists to ensure that the platform serves as a public educational tool while maintaining clear separation between school responsibilities and independent educational work.
